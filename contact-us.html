Travel tips

A little planning helps you enjoy every trail and learn more about the natural environment.

Best time to visit

The driest months are January to February and June to October. These are the most comfortable months for hiking and the best for wildlife.

Getting here

Most trips start from our Karen office or a meeting point we share when you confirm. From Nairobi, the drive takes 25 to 60 minutes depending on traffic.

Visitor guidelines

  • Stay with your guide and on the trail
  • Keep a respectful distance from wildlife
  • Carry out all your rubbish
  • Ask before photographing people
  • Never feed animals

In an emergency anywhere in Kenya, call 999 or 112. Our guides carry radios and first-aid kits on every trip.

Questions we hear often

Do I need to be very fit?

Not for most experiences. The Ngong Hills walk and the wildlife morning suit most people. Mount Longonot needs good fitness.

Can children join?

Children over 10 can join the Ngong Hills walk, and all ages are welcome on tree-planting days with an adult.

What if the weather is bad?

Your guide will shorten, delay or change the route if conditions are unsafe.

Where does my fee go?

It pays guides fairly, covers running costs, and 10% goes to conservation projects and host communities.
