Welcome to Safari Crest

Safari Crest is an organization that promotes responsible ecotourism and nature conservation in Kenya. We organize guided hiking and wildlife experiences, support local communities living near conservation areas, and educate visitors about protecting the natural environment.

Services we offer:


  • Guided hiking tours
  • Wildlife experiences
  • Community support programs
  • Educate visitors about conservation
